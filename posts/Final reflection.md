VintaArchive is a Sydney-based vintage platform that combines themed exhibitions with a peer-to-peer marketplace. The project was designed to help users discover, share, and exchange vintage objects through storytelling. This final reflection evaluates the prototype through performance, user experience, accessibility, functional requirements, and lessons learned during development.
